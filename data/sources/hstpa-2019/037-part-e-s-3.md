# Part E, § 3: This act shall take effect immediately; provided, further, that the amendments to section 26-511 of chapter 4… (PDF p. 8)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 8]
§ 3. This act shall take effect immediately; provided, further, that
the amendments to section 26-511 of chapter 4 of title 26 of the admin-
istrative code of the city of New York made by section two of this act
shall expire on the same date as such law expires and shall not affect
the expiration of such law as provided under section 26-520 of such law.
