# Part M, § 13, Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law (evictions): Section 731 of the real property actions and proceedings law (PDF p. 48)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 48]
§ 13. Section 731 of the real property actions and proceedings law is
amended by adding a new subdivision 4 to read as follows:
4. In an action premised on a tenant defaulting in the payment of
rent, payment to the landlord of the full amount of rent due, when such
payment is made at any time prior to the hearing on the petition, shall
be accepted by the landlord and renders moot the grounds on which the
special proceeding was commenced.
