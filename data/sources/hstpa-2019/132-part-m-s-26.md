# Part M, § 26: Subdivision 1 of section 212 of the judiciary law (PDF p. 56-57)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 56]
§ 26. Subdivision 1 of section 212 of the judiciary law is amended by
adding a new paragraph (x) to read as follows:
(x) Not permit the unified court system to sell any data regarding
judicial proceedings related to residential tenancy, rent or eviction to
any third party. Such prohibition includes data collected, stored or
[PDF page 57]

utilized by any third-party vendors who have contracts with the unified
court system.
