# Part F, § 7: This act shall take effect immediately and shall apply to any claims pending or filed on and after such date… (PDF p. 15)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 15]
§ 7. This act shall take effect immediately and shall apply to any
claims pending or filed on and after such date; provided that the amend-
ments to section 26-516 of chapter 4 of title 26 of the administrative
code of the city of New York made by sections four and five of this act
shall expire on the same date as such law expires and shall not affect
the expiration of such law as provided under section 26-520 of such law.
