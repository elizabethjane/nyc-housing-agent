# Part M, § 232-b: Notification to terminate monthly tenancy or tenancy from month to month outside the city of New York. A month… (PDF p. 45)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 45]
§ 232-b. Notification to terminate monthly tenancy or tenancy from
month to month outside the city of New York. A monthly tenancy or tenan-
cy from month to month of any lands or buildings located outside of the
city of New York may be terminated by the [landlord or the] tenant upon
[his] the tenant's notifying the [other] landlord at least one month
before the expiration of the term of [his] the tenant's election to
terminate; provided, however, that no notification shall be necessary to
terminate a tenancy for a definite term.
