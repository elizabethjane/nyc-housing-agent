# Part G, § 14: Application of act. The provisions of this act shall [only] be applicable: a. in the city of New York; and b.… (PDF p. 16)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 16]
§ 14. Application of act. The provisions of this act shall [only] be
applicable:
a. in the city of New York; and
b. in [the counties of Nassau, Westchester and Rockland] all counties
within the state of New York outside the city of New York and shall
become and remain effective only in a city, town or village located
therein as provided in section three of this act.
