# Part A, § 4: This act shall take effect immediately; provided, that the provisions of sections one and three of this act sh… (PDF p. 4)

> 2019 bill text as passed. Text in [brackets] was deleted by the act; added text is not marked in this copy. This act is the approved source for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled rules, which the NY Senate snapshots do not include.

[PDF page 4]
§ 4. This act shall take effect immediately; provided, that the
provisions of sections one and three of this act shall remain in full
force and effect [only until and including June 15, 2019] thereafter;
and provided further that any plan accepted for filing by the department
of law on or before the effective date of this act shall continue to be
governed by the provisions of section 352-eee of the general business
law as they had existed immediately prior to the effective date of this
act.
