# Does an owner have a right to collect a security deposit?

Yes, at the initial renting of the apartment to the tenant, the owner may collect a security deposit. The amount of a [security deposit](https://www.nyc.gov/content/tenantprotection/pages/protections-for-all-tenants) can be no more than one month’s rent. The security deposit must be kept by the owner in an interest bearing account in a NYS bank. For further details, see HCR [Fact Sheet #9: Renting an Apartment – Security Deposits and Other Charges](https://hcr.ny.gov/fact-sheet-9).
