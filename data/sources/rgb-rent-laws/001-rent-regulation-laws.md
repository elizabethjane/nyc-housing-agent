# Rent Regulation Laws

The following are the five most recent iterations of New York rent laws impacting rent regulated tenants in New York since 1997:

- [Rent Regulation Reform Act of 1997](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/rent-regulation-laws/rent-regulation-reform-act-of-1997/)

- [Rent Law of 2003](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/rent-regulation-laws/rent-law-of-2003/)

- [Rent Act of 2011](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/rent-regulation-laws/rent-act-of-2011/)

- [Rent Act of 2015](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/rent-regulation-laws/rent-act-of-2015/)

- [Rent Laws of 2019](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/resources/rent-regulation-laws/rent-laws-of-2019/) (Housing Stability & Tenant Protection Act of 2019)

For additional reference:

- The Laws of New York / Consolidated Laws / Real Property, Article 7 (Landlord and Tenant) can be found on the [NYS Senate website](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/A7).
