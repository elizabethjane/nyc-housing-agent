# Apartment/Loft Orders & Explanatory Statements

NYC Rent Guidelines Board Apartment and Loft Guidelines apply to leases for rent stabilized apartments and lofts located in New York City.

For a summary chart of apartment rent guidelines orders enacted by the RGB, download the [Apartment Summary Chart](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RGB-Apartment-Chart.pdf) of RGB Apartment Rent Guidelines Orders effective through Sept. 30, 2027.

For vacancy lease rent increase information, see the [Vacancy Leases section](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/rent-guidelines/vacancy-leases/).

For information on rent stabilized apartments outside of NYC, please contact the [NYS Homes and Community Renewal (HCR)](https://hcr.ny.gov/office-rent-administration-ora#contact-ora).

Apartment/Loft Orders and Explanatory Statements from Order #42 to the present are available below. Apartment/Loft Orders #17-#41 (but not Explanatory Statements) are also available below. If you would like copies of Orders or Explanatory Statements not available online, please [contact us](https://rentguidelinesboard.cityofnewyork.us/about/email-the-rgb/).
