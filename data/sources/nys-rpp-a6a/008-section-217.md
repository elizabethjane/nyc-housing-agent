# § 217 Preservation of existing requirements of law

* § 217. Preservation of existing requirements of law. No action shall
be maintainable and no judgment of possession shall be entered for
housing accommodations pursuant to section two hundred sixteen of this
article, unless the landlord has complied with any and all applicable
laws governing such action or proceeding and has complied with any and
all applicable laws governing notice to tenants, including without
limitation the manner and the time of service of such notice and the
contents of such notice.
* NB Repealed June 15, 2034
