# Late Fees

Late fees can only be charged if rent is received more than five days after the due date established in the lease, and cannot exceed $50 or five percent of the rent, whichever is less.

*The 14 day return of a security deposit does not apply to rent regulated tenants.

These are just several of the key changes in the new rent laws. To learn more: [visit New York State Division of Housing and Community Renewal (DHCR](https://hcr.ny.gov/division-housing-and-community-renewal)) or email DHCR's Tenant Protection Unit: tpuinfo@nyshcr.org.
