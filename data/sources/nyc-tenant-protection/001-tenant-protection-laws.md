# Tenant Protection Laws

## Tenant Protection Laws

All NYC tenants are entitled to basic rights. [The Tenant Bill of Rights](https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/services-and-information/tenant-bill-of-rights.page) is a collaboration between HPD and the Tenant Protection Cabinet. It is not a substitute for advice from a lawyer or legal representation. If you are a tenant seeking support, call 311 and ask for the Tenant Helpline.

Tenants in New York have a strong set of rights that protect them in interactions with landlords or managing agents.

The rent laws passed in 2019 by lawmakers in Albany made it harder for landlords to evict all tenants and strengthened protections for New Yorkers living in rent regulated apartments. These laws are enforced by the State's Division of Homes and Community Renewal (DHCR). DHCR is the State's affordable housing agency, with a mission to build, preserve, and protect affordable housing and increase home ownership throughout New York State.[1](https://www.nyc.gov/content/tenantprotection/pages/tenant-protection-laws#footnote-1)
