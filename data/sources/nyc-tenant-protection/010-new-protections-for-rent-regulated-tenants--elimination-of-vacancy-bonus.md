# Elimination of Vacancy Bonus

Prior to June 14, 2019, owners of rent stabilized apartments were awarded up to a 20% bonus to the "legal" rent in between tenancies. Today that bonus has been eliminated.

Did you recently move into a rent stabilized apartment? Find out what the last tenant paid and make sure you aren't being overcharged by getting your rent history! To get your rent history, contact New York State Division of Housing and Community Renewal's (DHCR) Rent Administration office at 718-739-6400 or you can access your rent history by using "[Rent Info - Submit a Question](https://portal.hcr.ny.gov/app/ask)".
