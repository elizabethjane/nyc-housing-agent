# Reversing Evictions

Many tenants are evicted for non-payment of rent. Changes to the law make it easier for families to reverse eviction decisions for non-payment of rent if they are able to come up with the money before the city marshal arrives. The court has the discretion to vacate the warrant and restore the tenant to possession subsequent to the execution of the warrant of eviction for good cause.

If you are in this situation, New York City may be able to help. [Visit NYC's Human Resources Administration](https://www1.nyc.gov/site/hra/help/rental-assistance.page ) to see if you are eligible for a one-time subsidy to help pay back rent.
