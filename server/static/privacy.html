Privacy

You don't need an account. Please don't share your name, address, phone number, or Social Security number; the agent doesn't need them.

What is kept

  • Your messages, the agent's replies, which sources it looked up, and any thumbs up or down you give.
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, and Social Security numbers are removed before anything is saved.
  • Household size and income are kept, because they are needed to check that lottery answers are correct.
  • A cookie that keeps your conversation together. It identifies the conversation, not you.

How long

Conversation records are deleted after 30 days.

Who sees it

Messages are sent to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model, so it can write the replies. They are not sold or shared with anyone else. Records are used only to check and improve the agent's answers.
