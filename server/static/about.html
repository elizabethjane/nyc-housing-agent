About

NYC Housing Agent is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of New York, the NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD), or the NYC Rent Guidelines Board.

It gives information, not legal advice. For help with your situation, see the NYC tenant protection page or call 311 and ask for the Tenant Helpline.

What it covers

  • NYC and New York State rental laws, for apartments in the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, and Staten Island.
  • Open NYC affordable housing lotteries you qualify for, and how to apply on Housing Connect.

Where answers come from

Only these sources, each linked in the answers that use them:

  • NYC Housing Connect (open lotteries, refreshed every few hours)
  • NYC Rent Guidelines Board: rent regulation laws, the rent laws of 2019, apartment orders, and the security deposit FAQ
  • NYC tenant protection laws
  • NY Real Property Law, Article 7 and Article 6-A (Good Cause Eviction)
  • NYS Homes and Community Renewal: Good Cause Eviction
  • HPD/HDC asset limits and property ownership rules for lottery applicants
  • NYS Attorney General: Rent Security Complaint Form

Lottery matches are based on household size and income. Each unit also has an asset limit, and there are rules about owning property; the agent can explain both. Other requirements are listed on each lottery's Housing Connect page.
